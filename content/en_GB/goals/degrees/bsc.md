I started my undergraduate degree in Mathematics & Physics in 2017 at the [University of Aberdeen](https://abdn.ac.uk). After spending two years there, during the beginning of the pandemic, I moved my studies to the [Open University](https://open.ac.uk) where I studied Mathematics, and later, Cybersecurity.

In 2022, before completing my degree, I got the opportunity to live and work abroad in Québec, Canada, which I did for two years, until late 2024. I placed my studies on pause while doing so.

As I find myself back in the UK, I am preparing to complete my remaining undergraduate credits through studies in Mathematics & Computer Science — two areas of personal interest to me. I also plan to use this degree to enrol in a Master's program in [[mlis|Library & Information Sciences]].
