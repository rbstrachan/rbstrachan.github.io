Following the completion of my [[bsc|undergraduate degree]], I intend to pursue a Master’s degree in Library & Information Sciences ([MLIS](https://sheffield.ac.uk/postgraduate/taught/courses/2026/librarianship-ma) or [MSI](https://admission.umontreal.ca/programmes/maitrise-en-sciences-de-linformation)), likely at either the Université de Montréal in Canada or the University of Sheffield in the UK.

Particularly, I am interested in learning and working in areas such as information organization and architecture, digital archives and preservation, digital remastering, open access and tactile accessibility.

I intend to research [[braille-implementations|the differences in the implementation of Braille across international writing systems]].
