This folder contains notes detailing and tracking my long-term goals of building strong reading ability and kanji knowledge in Japanese.

I’m currently aiming for the [<ruby>日<rt>に</rt>本<rt>ほん</rt>語<rt>ご</rt>能<rt>のう</rt>力<rt>りょく</rt>試<rt>し</rt>験<rt>けん</rt></ruby> (JLPT)]() N2 as my main goal. I may or may not pursue N1 later, depending on how useful it feels and whether the extra effort is worth it for me.

In parallel, I also want to work toward the [<ruby>漢<rt>かん</rt>字<rt>じ</rt>検定<rt>けんてい</rt>]() 2級 (Kanji Kentei Grade 2), which is a kanji-focused exam that tests reading, writing, vocabulary and correct written usage of official kanji. Unlike the JLPT it is not a broad comprehension exam. Instead, it focuses much more on detailed and intricate kanji knowledge and written language accuracy.

Both the JLPT and the Kanken treat Level 1 as the hardest and higher number levels, such as 2, 3, 4, etc., as easier. 

## Why this matters to me
Japanese is one of my main long-term learning goals. I want enough skill to read comfortably, understand more nuanced writing, and keep building toward advanced literacy. I especially enjoy reading and studying Japanese kanji, hence the interest in the 漢検.
