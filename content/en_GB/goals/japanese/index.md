This folder contains notes detailing and tracking my progress toward my long-term goal of building strong reading, writing and speaking ability and kanji knowledge in Japanese.

I’m currently aiming for the [<ruby>日<rt>に</rt>本<rt>ほん</rt>語<rt>ご</rt>能<rt>のう</rt>力<rt>りょく</rt>試<rt>し</rt>験<rt>けん</rt></ruby> (JLPT)](goals/japanese/jlpt) <span class="small-caps">N2</span> as my main goal. I may or may not pursue <span class="small-caps">N1</span> later, depending on how useful it feels and whether the extra effort is worth it for me.

In parallel, I also want to work toward the [<ruby>漢<rt>かん</rt>字<rt>じ</rt>検定<rt>けんてい</rt>](goals/japanese/kanken) 2級 (Kanji Kentei Grade 2), which is a kanji-focused exam that tests reading, writing, vocabulary and correct written usage of official kanji. Unlike the JLPT, it is not a broad comprehension exam. Instead, it focuses much more on detailed and intricate kanji knowledge and written language accuracy.

Both the JLPT and the Kanken treat Level 1 as the hardest and higher number levels, such as 2, 3, 4, etc., as easier.

## Why am I doing this?
Japanese is one of my main long-term learning goals. I want enough skill to read comfortably and understand more nuanced writing to continue building toward advanced literacy. I especially enjoy reading and studying kanji in particular, hence the interest in the 漢検.

If, in the future, I get the opportunity to study for a masters degree in Japan, I would love to do so and it would be great to be able to do that in Japanese.
