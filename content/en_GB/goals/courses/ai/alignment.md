An intensive, 12 week educational programme by [BlueDot Impact](https://bluedot.org/courses/alignment) focused on the technical challenges of ensuring advanced artificial intelligence systems remain safe, controllable and aligned with human intent.

Covering core machine learning safety concepts such as reward learning, reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), inner and outer alignment, goal misgeneralisation, mechanistic interpretability and technical evaluation frameworks for frontier models; the AI Alignment course provides a comprehensive grounding in current research directions surrounding machine learning safety, interpretability and governance of technical risk through multiple hours of weekly readings, technical assignments and facilitated seminar discussions.

Graduates of this course can demonstrate technical competence in assessing AI failure modes, reviewing empirical alignment literature and evaluating risk-mitigation strategies within machine learning research.
