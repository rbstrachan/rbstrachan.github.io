The MLH Fellowship in Software Engineering is an intensive, 12-week fellowships where members contribute to open-source projects, collaborate in global pods and gain real-world engineering experience with mentorship from [MLH](https://fellowship.mlh.io/).

> A remote internship alternative for aspiring software engineers where you build project experience with open source and real-world tools.  
> ― *Major League Hacking*
