## Charitable services
The following charities and services perform vital work to protect <abbr title="lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer and questioning">LGBTQ+</abbr> people of all ages, and their rights, internationally. If you can, please consider making a donation.

### United States
- [The Trevor Project](https://give.thetrevorproject.org/campaign/797408/donate) provides crisis support and suicide prevention for young LGBTQ+ people.
- [The Marsha P. Johnson Institute](https://givebutter.com/MPJI-pride) defends the human rights of Black transgender people.

### United Kingdom
- [Stonewall UK](https://www.stonewall.org.uk/get-involved/support-us/ways-to-donate-to-stonewall/regular-donation) drives legislative change and fights for daily legal protections across the UK.
- [Stonewall Housing](https://stonewallhousing.org/donate/) provides safe housing, legal advice and mental health support for LGBTQ+ people facing homelessness.
- [Mermaids](https://mermaidsuk.org.uk/?form=donate) supports trans, non-binary and gender-diverse youth and their families.

### Europe
- [ILGA-Europe](https://rainbowmap.ilga-europe.org/support/) supports over 700 grassroots activist groups across Europe and publishes the annual Rainbow Map tracking legal human rights.
- [TGEU (Transgender Europe)](https://tgeu.org/support-our-work/) acts as a powerful trans-led network advocating for healthcare accessibility and legal gender recognition across Europe.

### International
- [Rainbow Railroad](https://www.rainbowrailroad.org/donate) helps at-risk LGBTQ+ individuals escape violence and state-sponsored persecution by facilitating emergency relocation to safer countries.
- [Outright International](https://outrightinternational.org/?form=GiveOutDay26) monitors and defends human rights globally, supporting grassroots activists and documenting discrimination on six continents.
- [ILGA World](https://donorbox.org/support-the-ilga-world-database) operates as a global federation of over 2,000 member organizations across 170 countries, campaigning for equal rights and legal protections at the United Nations.
- [GiveOut](https://giveout.org/donate-giveout/) is an international grant-making charity that pools donations to directly fund and resource other under-funded LGBTQ+ activist groups worldwide.
