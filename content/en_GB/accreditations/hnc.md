The [Higher National Certificate (HNC) in Applied Sciences](https://www.nescol.ac.uk/courses/hnc-applied-science-chemistry-physics-route/) is an SCQF Level 7 (first year undergraduate equivalent) course at North East Scotland College (NESCOL), including coursework in organic chemistry, physics, mathematics and laboratory skills. The final course assessment was a graded unit consisting of an independent scientific research project and dissertation.

I completed and passed this qualification in 2017, providing me with a practical foundation in core scientific principles, laboratory techniques and analytical methods.
