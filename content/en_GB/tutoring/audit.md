## During the call
The call will be an intensive 60-minute session structured to act as a diagnostic for your language learning setup. We'll go over:

- deconstruct your current study habits to find blind spots
- do a real-time proficiency check to see where you actually sit on the A1–C2 scale
- identify which method(s) (visual, auditory, tactile) align with how your brain actually learns and processes information
- help you filter through the noise to find the high-quality, free open resources that match your level
- give you concrete, actionable advice on how to progress towards your goal from your current level
- **your language learning habits** including your current study routine to find and fix hidden blind spots, operational friction, and fossilised mistakes
- **Real-Time Proficiency Check:** We will evaluate your true active level across the four core skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking) against the CEFR scale.
- **Cognitive Interface Alignment:** We’ll identify whether your study methods align with how your brain actually processes information (visual scaffolding, spatial mapping, or sensory input calibration).
- **Resource Curation:** Cut through the internet's noise. I'll help you filter and select high-quality, open-access resources tailored precisely to your current plateau.
- **The Strategic Roadmap:** You'll leave with concrete, prioritized, and actionable steps to bridge the gap between where you are and where you want to be.

## After the call
Within 72 hours of our session, you will receive an exhaustive, bespoke PDF report detailing our findings, diagnostic breakdowns, mechanical exercises, and your 6-month milestone roadmap.

[[ai|I do not use AI]]. Every report is hand-crafted based on professional TEFL training, higher-education pedagogy, years of classroom experience, personal mastery of a second language at a native level, and the specific data gathered during our 1-on-1 conversation.

Learning a language to fluency is a long-term investment. Doing it efficiently prevents you from wasting hundreds of hours on outdated methods and tactics that hurt more than they help. An upfront audit pays for itself by optimising every minute of your future self-study.

Language acquisition isn't a one-and-done thing. To ensure your strategy remains tailored as you evolve, a follow-up check-up call is available at a **60% discount**. This is ideal if you believe your abilities have increased significantly (typically 6 to 12 months after your initial audit) and you want to review your progress, update your toolkit and rethink your strategy for the next stages of fluency.
