dotDotDecode is a fairly simple tool to test and train your visual [[Braille]] reading ability and speed.
I made this hastily while I was learning to read [[Braille]] by sight myself, mainly as a way to practice and get used to seeing [[Braille]] in type.

I've published it here in hopes that it may help someone else do the same or serve as an easy way to promote the learning and understanding of [[Braille]] in general.

## How to play
Choose your difficulty by adjusting the slider in the top left corner. A sentence, written in a [[Braille]] font, will then be displayed. Your goal is to type out the correct English text (including punctuation) in the text box at the bottom. The textbox will highlight itself red if you make a mistake.
