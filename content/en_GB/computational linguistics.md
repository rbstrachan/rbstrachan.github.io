In the study of linguistics, computational linguistics is an interdisciplinary field that combines linguistics and computer science to analyse and understand human language using computational methods.

There are two main applications for computational linguistics, (a) helping computers process and generate language as humans do, through tasks such as machine translation, speech recognition and sentiment analysis; and (b) helping linguists discover the underlying patterns, structures and evolutionary properties of a language by, for example, running statistical analyses on massive text corpora to track how its dialects have changed over time.
