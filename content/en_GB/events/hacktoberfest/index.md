title: Hacktoberfest
---

Hacktoberfest is an annual, month-long celebration held every October by [MLH](https://mlh.com) and [DEV](https://dev.to) comprising of more than 300 community-hosted meetups and hands-on hack days, livestreamed workshops, virtual events, mini-games, weekly coding challenges and competitions. Participants unlock virtual stickers and digital collectibles by completing various challenges throughout the month.

This folder contains notes and source code for my contributions to Hacktoberfest coding challenges, including the DEV Challenges (Launch Weekend + Weeks 1–4) and Global Hack Week.
