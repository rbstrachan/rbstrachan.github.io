---
title: Binderary
---

Binderary is an annual, month-long celebration of bookbinding and all its disciplines, from papercraft and leatherworking to repair and structural design. Taking place every February, Binderary is filled with hands-on workshops, creative challenges and community events hosted by the [Renegade Bookbinding Guild](https://www.renegadeguild.org/).
