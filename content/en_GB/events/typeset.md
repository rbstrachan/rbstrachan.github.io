title: Renegade Bindery Typesetting Exchange
---

The Renegade Bindery Typesetting Exchange is a collaborative community event where members of the [Renegade Bindery Discord](https://discord.gg/3mBP9ZRHAc) design custom book layouts to gift to one another for printing and binding.

I am participating in this year's exchange! This is my first-ever time typesetting a book from zero. I understand typesetting is an art and I'm excited to learn from some of the best.

> [!info] Check back soon!
> For fear of spoiling the surprise for my gift exchange partner, I can't yet reveal the title of the text I'm working on. However, once the exchange concludes and all typesets have been safely and securely delivered, I will publish the completed typeset for download on the 20th November. In accordance with the Guild's gift economy, the typeset will be free to download so anyone interested can print or bind it.
