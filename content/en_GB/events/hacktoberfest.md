title: Hacktoberfest
---

Hacktoberfest is an annual, month-long celebration of code held every October by [MLH](https://mlh.com) and [DEV](https://dev.to) comprising of more than 300 community-hosted meetups and hands-on hack days, livestreamed workshops, virtual events, mini-games, weekly coding challenges and competitions.

Participants can unlock virtual stickers and digital collectibles by completing various challenges throughout the month.
